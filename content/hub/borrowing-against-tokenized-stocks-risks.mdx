The live markets are on the [Borrow page](/borrow). Read this before opening a loan against any of
them.

## Max LTV is not a target

It is the line at which you are liquidated, not a sensible borrowing level. A position opened near
the maximum is liquidated by an ordinary day's move in the share price.

## The weekend is the real risk

The share stops trading on Friday; the token does not. An oracle still has to quote a price, and the
gap when the market reopens can liquidate a position nobody was awake to defend. This risk does not
exist when you hold the share itself.

## The lend side is a different trade

Morpho's interface shows a "Lend Rate" on each of these markets, and some are high. It is not yield
on your stock: in a Morpho Blue market the lender supplies the loan asset and the collateral is never
lent out. To earn it you deposit USDG or USDC, not shares.

## A cheap rate usually means a quiet market

Borrow rates fall when nobody is borrowing. The lowest numbers sit in markets holding a few hundred
thousand dollars, where your own loan would move the rate.
