There are two routes. Which one you need depends on what you want to do with the token afterwards.

## Route 1: an exchange or app account

Some crypto exchanges and broker apps list tokenized stocks next to their other assets. You sign up,
pass the usual identity checks, deposit money and buy, much like a broker.

- **Good for:** buying and holding, and any rate the platform itself pays inside its account.
- **Limit:** the tokens sit in the platform's custody. To use them anywhere else, you have to
  withdraw them to your own wallet (Route 2).

## Route 2: your own wallet

A **wallet** is an app that holds the keys to your tokens. With a self-custody wallet, nobody else
can move your assets, and nobody can recover them if you lose your recovery phrase. Treat that phrase
like the only key to a safe.

The steps are:

1. **Install a wallet for the right blockchain.** Wallets are built for one family of chains or
   several. Check that yours supports the chain the token lives on.
2. **Fund it.** Buy a stablecoin (a dollar token, such as USDC) on an exchange and withdraw it to
   your wallet. Also hold a small amount of the chain's own coin to pay network fees.
3. **Swap for the stock token** on a decentralised exchange, or withdraw stock tokens you already
   bought on an exchange.
4. **Check the contract address.** Anyone can create a token called "NVDA". Only buy the one whose
   address matches the issuer's published address. Every stock page on this site lists only tokens
   whose address we have verified against the issuer.

- **Good for:** every opportunity on this site: lending pools, trading pools and borrowing.

## The chain decides where you can go

Each tokenized stock lives on a specific blockchain: Ethereum, Solana, Base, BNB Chain, Robinhood
Chain and others. Platforms only accept tokens on the chains they run on. So before you buy, decide
what you want to do with the token, and buy it on that chain.

Every row on the [Lend](/earn), [Market-make](/liquidity) and [Borrow](/borrow) pages shows its chain.
The [stock pages](/stocks) group every token and market for one company.

## Costs to expect

- **Exchange fees and spreads** when you buy.
- **Network fees** for every transaction from a wallet: usually cents on Solana and Base, more on
  Ethereum.
- **Withdrawal fees** when moving tokens off an exchange.
