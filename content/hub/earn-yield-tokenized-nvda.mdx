NVDA is the most widely tokenized stock onchain, which means there are several different NVDA
tokens and they do not go to the same places. Here is the current map.

<LiveTable ticker="NVDA" />

## The tokens are not interchangeable

Four issuers wrap the same share, and your choice of wrapper decides which venues you can reach:

- **NVDAx** (xStocks, Solana) — the most liquid wrapper. Accepted by Kamino's xStocks Market and
  Jupiter Lend.
- **NVDAc** (Coinbase, Base) — the deposit asset for Superform's superNVDA vault and for the
  Aave V4 Equities Hub.
- **NVDA** (Robinhood, Robinhood Chain) — has a Morpho market, collateral only.

Moving between them means selling one and buying another. There is no bridge between wrappers.

## What each venue actually does with it

**Kamino and Jupiter Lend** lend it out. That is the only category here that can pay real
interest — and it currently pays almost none, because tokenized NVDA borrow demand is
negligible. On 19 October 2026, Kamino's NVDAx market paid 0.02% and Jupiter's paid 0.00%.

**Superform's superNVDA** deposits your NVDAc, borrows USDC against it on Aave and loops. On
19 October 2026 it showed 16.97%, of which 0.17 percentage points was interest and 16.80 was
token incentives. It is the highest number on the page and also the only one with a liquidation
price.

**Aave V4's Equities Hub and the Morpho markets** take NVDA as collateral and never lend it.
They pay 0%. What you get is USDC borrowing power, not yield.

## The risks that are specific to this asset

- **Oracle gaps.** NVDA trades on Nasdaq during market hours; the token trades always. Any
  leveraged position has to survive whatever the oracle quotes over a weekend and the reopening
  gap on Monday.
- **Issuer structure.** You hold a claim on an issuer, not a share. Redemption rules differ by
  issuer and by your country of residence.
- **Thin markets.** Several of these pools hold low six figures. A rate on a small pool is not a
  rate you can get in size.

## The short version

If you want interest on tokenized NVDA, there is barely any to be had. If you want the incentive
yield, understand that you are taking leverage and a liquidation price to get it. If someone
quotes you a number, ask which part is base.
