## Fees are real yield

Trading fees are paid by traders on every swap, not by a marketing budget. That makes this the one
category where the rate on a tokenized stock can be large and durable — for as long as people keep
trading the pair.

## Who keeps the fees

Not always the LP. Raydium keeps 16% of every fee, and StockAPY's rates are after that cut —
Raydium's own figure is not. Uniswap charges a protocol fee on some pools, not deducted in our
figures. Aero sends 5% of unstaked LPs' fees to sAERO Token Operators, which we deduct; LPs who stake
for AERO emissions give up the fees entirely.

## Impermanent loss

Put $500 of NVDA and $500 of USDC in a pool. If NVDA rises 30%, the pool rebalances by selling NVDA:
you end with about 12% fewer shares and hold $1,140, against $1,150 had you just held — a $10
shortfall your fees have to cover. Bigger moves cost far more, and concentrated positions magnify it.

## Markets close, pools don't

The stock exchange shuts on Friday; the pool keeps trading. Its price drifts on thin weekend flow,
and when the market reopens arbitrageurs trade it back to the real price — against the liquidity
providers. This gap is specific to tokenized stocks.

## Concentrated liquidity

On Uniswap v3 and v4, Raydium CLMM and Aero CL pools you earn only while the price stays inside the
range you chose. The APY shown assumes you stay in range; out of range you earn nothing and hold only
the losing side.

## Busy today, quiet tomorrow

We list only pools that traded at least $1,000 in the last 24 hours and hold $10k or more. A day's
volume still swings: a pool on a heavy news day can show a rate it will not repeat. Pairs against
memecoins are flagged speculative — you would hold half your position in that token.
